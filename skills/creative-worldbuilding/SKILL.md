# Creative Worldbuilding

Develop concrete material that fits the brief and creates possibilities for scenes. Let the requested genre, tone, and intensity guide the design.

- Worlds: make rules, institutions, places, and histories affect people's choices. Give systems consequences and keep their internal logic coherent.
- Characters: make desire, contradiction, voice, and relationships visible through behavior. Design tendencies that can respond to a scene rather than scripting every outcome.
- Scale detail to the character's role and the requested use. Build on an accepted direction; offer distinct alternatives when the user is exploring.
- For runtime-ready roleplay material, distinguish persistent context, conditional lore, and scene setup using the target runtime's verified format.

Deliver the requested concept, reference, character sheet, or revision. Include examples or an opener when they help fulfill that request.
